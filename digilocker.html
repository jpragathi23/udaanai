DigiLocker

DigiLocker is a secure digital platform provided by the Government of India where students can store and access important educational documents such as:

  • 10th / 12th Mark Sheets
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Scholarship Certificates
  • Internship Offer Letters
  • Degree / Diploma Certificates

You can link your Aadhaar number and create a DigiLocker account in just a few minutes. It is now widely accepted for document verification across universities, companies, and government exams.
