NPTEL Courses

NPTEL (National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning) is an initiative by IITs and IISc to offer online courses with certification. These courses help students from 5th grade to PhD-level in technical and non-technical subjects.

Benefits of NPTEL:

  • Free access to high-quality content by IIT Professors
  • Certificates useful for college credits and job placements
  • Wide range of courses (engineering, humanities, school-level)
